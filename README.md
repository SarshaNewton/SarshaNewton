# Hey, I'm Sarsha! 🪐

I'm a **developer, designer, and curious maker** from Grenada 🇬🇩, currently pursuing my Master's in **Human-Computer Interaction and Design (HCID)** at KTH Royal Institute of Technology through the 28DIGITAL (formerly EIT Digital) Master School.

With a Bachelor's in Computer Science, I like working at the intersection of **engineering, design, and human behaviour**. I enjoy figuring out not just *how* to build something, but *why* it should exist, who it's for, and how people will actually experience it.

My projects tend to start with a question, an inconvenience, or an idea that gets a little out of hand.

## What I'm exploring 🔭

- **Human-Computer Interaction:** UX research, interaction design, usability, and the relationship between people and technology.
- **Creative Development:** Building interactive experiences that combine functional engineering with thoughtful visual design.
- **AI & Emerging Technologies:** Exploring how AI influences our workflows, decision-making, and everyday interactions.
- **Making Things:** Experimenting with physical and digital prototypes, occasionally just because I think they'd be fun to build.

## Selected projects 🚀

### [FADE — Secure, AI-Assisted Email Client](https://github.com/SarshaNewton/Fade)
My Bachelor's thesis project, exploring how security insights and AI-assisted email management can help people navigate their inboxes with greater clarity and confidence.

Built with Laravel, Livewire, Alpine.js, PostgreSQL, and Docker, with a focus on usable security and reducing cognitive load.

### [Mini Sarsha — A Little Piece of Me, Digitally](https://sarshanewton.github.io/Portfolio/projects/sarshas-lab/mini-sarsha.html)
A physical-digital experiment combining custom-designed acrylic keychains, NFC technology, and an interactive web experience.

Part creative project, part social experiment, and entirely an excuse to make something.

### [Personal Portfolio — Mission Control](https://sarshanewton.github.io/Portfolio/)
A space-inspired portfolio I designed and developed to document my projects, experiments, and professional journey.

Because apparently a regular portfolio wasn't dramatic enough.
